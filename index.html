Our vision

Business software should be trusted, not tolerated.

Most small and mid-sized companies live between two bad options: spreadsheets that drift apart, or heavy ERPs that are slow, confusing and need consultants to change.

Divisent is the third option. A modern ERP that is fast on any device, explains itself in plain words, and is built so that the books cannot disagree with the shop floor. When a customer is invoiced, the stock, the receivable and the tax are already right.

We are a startup, and we build the way we want our own accountants to work: carefully, transparently, and with every rule written down.

The problem we solve

From scattered tools to one connected flow.

Why it's different

Rules that protect your data, enforced by the system, not by good intentions.

Nothing is silently edited

Posted entries and stock moves can't be changed or deleted. To undo, you reverse, and both lines stay visible forever.

No negative stock

The database itself refuses a delivery without available stock. Two people can never sell the same last unit.

Gap-free numbering

SO/2026/00001, SAL/2026/00007… Each document type has its own unbroken sequence per year.

Books that agree

A document's number is its journal entry's number. Ageing reports show a live "ledger matches" check against the accounts.

Double entry, always

Every posting balances. Debits equal credits, and Post stays disabled until a manual entry does too.

Archive, don't lose

Retire a customer or product without breaking old documents. Delete works only if nothing uses the record.
