Built for India GST

GST worked out as you bill.

Choose a tax code; Divisent applies CGST + SGST or IGST from the place of supply, checks GSTINs and carries HSN/SAC codes to the PDF.

  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B figures with checks, Excel and JSON
  • Input tax credit and reverse charge
  • GSTR-2B upload and matching
  • E-invoice and e-way bill files; IRN and QR on the PDF

File-based: you review and upload to the government portals yourself. There is no live portal connection, and generated JSON layouts should be verified before filing. VAT setups are supported too.

Questions, answered honestly.

Who is Divisent ERP for?

Growing trading and service businesses that want sales, stock and accounts in one place.

Does it file GST returns for me?

No. It prepares figures and files for you to check and upload. It is not connected live to the GST portal.

What isn't included yet?

Multi-currency, credit and debit notes, stock valuation, lot or serial tracking, bank reconciliation and payroll are not available today.

Can I run more than one company?

Yes. One login can work in several companies, and each company's data stays separate.

Can it be adapted to our process?

Yes, through custom fields and rules kept separate from the core, so the product stays upgradeable.
